## Definition

An actual reusable artifact — a checklist, template, schema, test set, glossary, or built tool — that someone else can lift and adapt without rebuilding it. A decision about how to structure something is a Decision, not a Toolkit Asset.

## How it is used

One of the five types every corpus unit is tagged with (`unit_type` on `units/`).

**What makes it reusable:** The artifact itself: what it is, what it's for, and what makes it liftable as-is.
