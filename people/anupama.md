## Involvement

The Product Charter's execution model names the **Program Team** as "David, Anupama + Team". The team owns stakeholder communication and the pathway content lifecycle (reviewing and publishing contributor submissions), onboards new adopters and relays user feedback to the Solution Team. It takes part in the weekly cross-team review. See [Program Team](../teams/program-team.md).

Anupama has no commits in the diffusion-cube repository. The charter gives no organisation or surname, so affiliation is left blank. In the app, the Program Team's review-and-publish job runs through the `admin` role ([admin](../roles/admin.md)) on `/admin`.
