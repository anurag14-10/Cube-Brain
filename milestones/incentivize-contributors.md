## Outcome

According to the Product Charter, users can give feedback on a pathway and acknowledge that they reused its know-how. Contributors can see that reuse and get insights from the questions other users asked about their pathways.

## Deliverables

- Feedback and reuse acknowledgement on a pathway.
- A contributor-facing view of reuse.
- Contributor insights from other users' questions.

## Status

`planned`. The charter gives no status for this milestone.
