## Outcome

According to the Product Charter, at least two EkStep teams, Voice/Language AI and Blue Dots, are actively using the Cube through the web app and/or Claude. They update their know-how and draw relevant learnings from others' work on an ongoing basis.

## Deliverables

- Two named EkStep teams in active use.
- The Claude Chat channel, which the charter's Scope says supports Explore, Analyse and Contribute without visiting the web app.

## Status

`planned`. The charter gives no status for this milestone. The [Blue Dots](../pathways/blue-dots.md) pathway is already in the corpus; that is corpus content, not evidence of the team's active use.
