## Outcome

According to the Product Charter, contributors and admins can see how a pathway is being used, where it may have gaps, and how much reuse is happening across pathways.

## Deliverables

The roadmap names four measures: the number of Explorers and Contributors, conversations per week, likes and dislikes, and reuse acknowledgements. Reuse acknowledgements depend on [Incentivize Contributors](incentivize-contributors.md).

## Status

`planned`. The charter gives no status for this milestone.
