## Core question

What has the institution seen fail publicly — and did they own it or disown it?

## Listening for

How the institution responds to the first failure is the strongest signal about whether this scales.

## Insight form

First public failure + institutional response (own vs disown)

## Corpus example

Bhili Language Enablement: in a live field demo, the helpline answered a farmer's cotton-pest question directly and declined a scheme question outright — "I will answer only farming-related questions" — rather than guessing to seem more capable.
