## Core question

Who else has tried to solve this for this population, and what happened?

## Listening for

Precedent deployments, failed attempts, adjacent tools. Who do they trust as a reference peer?

## Insight form

Named precedent + what transferred + what didn't

## Corpus example

Voice AI for Inclusion: the same architecture went from 9 months (MahaVISTAAR, built from scratch) to 3 months (Ethiopia's ATI) to 3 weeks (Amul/Sarlaben). The build compressed each time; trust-building and data partnerships still had to be redone locally.
