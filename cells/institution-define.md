## Core question

Who approves what the system says, have they agreed to own that, and what testing/timeline has the institution committed to before real users?

## Listening for

Content authority, not technical sign-off. Staged testing with named gate criteria.

## Insight form

Content authority + approval process + testing progression

## Corpus example

African Voice AI: testing runs through a named, gated sequence — internal team, then partner staff, then field agents, then real farmers — with no stage advancing until its own criterion is actually met, not just scheduled.
