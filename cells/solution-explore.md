## Core question

What channel or system are you replacing, what does it fail at, and is AI actually the right tool for that failure?

## Listening for

Two human callers, paper forms, nothing — plus honest reasoning on AI-fit vs simpler fixes.

## Insight form

Current channel + failure mode + AI-fit justification

## Corpus example

Blue Dots: an ITI field visit in Dharwad found four in five job seekers had no resume, and employers advertised on posters, poles, and auto-rickshaws — not portals. That redirected the team to voice before a line of code was written.
