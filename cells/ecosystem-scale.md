## Core question

What from your deployment could the next adopter reuse — with what conditions?

## Listening for

Not "we did X." "X works when Y is true, fails when Z is true."

## Insight form

Transferable unit + condition tag (applies when / fails when)

## Corpus example

Nivesh Suvidha: schemas, pipelines, and governance approaches were designed from day one to be picked up by the next state or department — not one-off artefacts. Infrastructure built once and shared is a public good; built once and siloed is technical debt.
