## Core question

Has the institution absorbed this — budget line, named owner, review cadence — and does the system leave people more capable or more dependent?

## Listening for

Absorption vs "still the project team's problem." Workforce agency outcome.

## Insight form

Absorption indicators (budget + owner + review cadence) + agency outcome (capability vs dependency)

## Corpus example

African Voice AI: every contract in the programme ends in 2026 and the compute allocation expires that November — the team names "who maintains this SDK in three years" as a still-open funding and governance question, not a technical one.
