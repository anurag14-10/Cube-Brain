## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Design the interface so that using it is the only training a farmer needs — no separate training programme.

**Why:** The interface (a phone call) is simple enough that a farmer learns it in the act of calling and asking, rather than needing prior instruction.

**In this deployment:** Farmers called a number, asked a question, and got an answer — with no separate onboarding step before that.

- **Applies when:** The interface is simple enough (a phone call) that no separate onboarding step is needed.
