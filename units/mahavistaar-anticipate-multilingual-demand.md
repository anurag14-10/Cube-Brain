## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Plan for languages beyond the launch language before going live.

**Why:** Demand for additional languages surfaces after launch regardless; planning for it is materially cheaper than retrofitting it.

**In this deployment:** MahaVISTAAR launched Marathi-only; demand for Hindi, Bhili, and English emerged only after launch.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The target region has meaningful linguistic diversity even if the pilot targets one language.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

- **After:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

It tells an adopter in a linguistically diverse region to budget the language roadmap at design time even when the pilot is single-language.
