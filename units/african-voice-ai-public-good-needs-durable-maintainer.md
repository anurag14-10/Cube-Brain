## Unit

According to Crane AI Labs and the UNDP AI Hub for Sustainable Development's African Voice AI pathway, this is a strategic decision from the Voicera Africa deployment in Kenya.

**Decision.** Treat "who maintains this SDK in three years, and are they in the room now" as a governance and funding question to be resolved deliberately, not an afterthought to the technical build.

**Why.** An SDK becomes a genuine Digital Public Good only when someone reviews contributions, ships security fixes, updates models, and answers issues years out. The technical path to sector-agnosticism is tractable; who owns the SDK in 2028 is not answered.

**Context.** The team's own honest position: every contract in the programme concludes at the end of 2026, and the supporting compute allocation expires in November 2026. The pathway names post-programme maintenance as the most common silent failure mode for this kind of deployment, and as unresolved here.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Building any infrastructure intended as a public good, particularly under grant or programme funding with a fixed end date.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

It moves the maintainer question from after the build to before it, for any adopter whose funding has a fixed end date. It is a suggestion from another adoption's lived experience, to be checked against the condition tag above.
