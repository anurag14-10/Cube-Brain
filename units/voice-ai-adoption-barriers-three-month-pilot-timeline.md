## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation / People+AI's Voice AI Adoption Barriers pathway, the asset is: a three-month pilot timeline: pre-pilot weeks 1–4 define the use case and capture a baseline; pilot weeks 5–9 build and run QA; post-pilot weeks 10–12 compare results to the baseline and make the go/no-go decision.

**Reuse condition:** Applies to any organisation running a first voice AI pilot and wanting a paced, dated structure rather than an open-ended trial.

Section 2 of the same pathway notes that closing the gap between a working demo and a working deployment reliably took three to four months of continuous in-house iteration in the organisations that succeeded.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** An organisation is running a first voice AI pilot and wants a paced, dated structure rather than an open-ended trial.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source
