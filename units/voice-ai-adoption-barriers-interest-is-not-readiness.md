## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation / People+AI's Voice AI Adoption Barriers pathway, this is a strategic decision drawn from the organisations it synthesises.

**Decision.** Treat "organisations say they're interested" and "organisations are ready to adopt" as two different populations requiring two different kinds of support, rather than assuming cheaper pricing and easier vendor access will convert one into the other.

**Alternative considered.** Subsidising price and vendor access (Listen at Scale: ~100,000 free minutes per organisation via Sarvam; Impact at Scale: ₹1/minute) on the hypothesis that cost and access are what hold adoption back.

**Why.** 86% of Listen at Scale's usage came from just 5 organisations; roughly 30 of 75 selected Impact at Scale organisations never went ahead with any vendor. The synthesis's five-tier funnel — deployed across the organisation, pilot in progress, actively exploring, aware but not exploring, not aware — showed most organisations well below "pilot in progress", and the two lower tiers are likely undercounted because the survey and interviews self-selected for organisations already aware of or exploring voice AI.

**Context.** Change management belongs in this same ecosystem-level category: Tryzent anticipated teacher resistance to a classroom voice AI tool and reframed the product — not as a replacement for the teacher, but as a way of surfacing which concepts students were struggling with.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Designing or evaluating a programme meant to accelerate voice AI adoption across many organisations, not building a single deployment.
- **Fails when:** The audience is a single, already-committed organisation — the funnel-segmentation logic doesn't apply to an audience of one.

## Before → after

- **Before:** Price and vendor access subsidised for 105 organisations on the assumption that removing them would convert interest into adoption; uptake stayed sharply lopsided regardless.
- **After:** Two interventions matched to two funnel positions — capacity-building and structured mentorship for organisations already piloting or actively exploring, lighter-touch awareness programmes for organisations never exposed to the technology.

## Why it is reusable

The condition tag says when this choice held across the organisations synthesised; the before→after shows what the other choice cost. It is a suggestion from other adoptions' lived experience, not a recommendation.
