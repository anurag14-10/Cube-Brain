## Unit

According to CEEW's Climate Intelligence pathway, this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Introduce AI as decision support — researchers keep interpreting climate evidence and officials keep making operational decisions — before embedding it into routine workflows.

**Alternative considered.** Not documented in the source

**Why.** Institutions trust systems they can understand, validate and improve — not systems that simply claim high accuracy; in both deployments AI adoption was ultimately a human process, not a technical one.

**Context.** Trust in CRAVIS.AI depended on keeping information grounded in credible, transparent sources; trust in the early-warning system depended on officials comparing forecasts against actual outcomes over time.

## Before → after

- **Before:** Not documented in the source
- **After:** Confidence was built through repeated operational use — for the early-warning system, by validating forecasts against real-world outcomes over successive cycles. AI changed how researchers and officials accessed information and forecasts, not who made the final call.
