## Unit

According to the Data DHARA pathway's account of Nivesh Suvidha (Invest UP), this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Budget explicitly for capacity building so the institution acquires data-management, prompt-engineering and system-maintenance capability, rather than relying on external vendors indefinitely.

**Alternative considered.** Relying on SIs, consultants and vendors without internal capability transfer.

**Why.** Without deliberate capacity building, institutions stay dependent on external vendors indefinitely, with no path to self-sufficiency.

**Context.** The document names, as drawbacks of AI adoption at state scale: no internal digital-transformation leadership (no Chief Digital Officer or Chief Data Officer to own the AI agenda); a capacity-building gap for 0-to-1 deployment; dependency on SIs, consultants and vendors with no internal capability transfer.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The deploying institution does not yet have the capabilities that building and sustaining an AI system requires — data management, prompt engineering, system maintenance.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

- **After:** Not documented in the source
