## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation's Bhili Language Enablement pathway, this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Let the effort be personally driven and coordinated by the District Collector, rather than delegated to a technical team or vendor.

**Alternative considered.** None documented. Every account of the project's origin, from the district side and from EkStep independently, converges on the collector's direct, hands-on involvement as the starting condition rather than an option weighed against others.

**Why.** District-level administrative authority was needed to converge multiple line departments (agriculture, education, health, tribal welfare) and to unlock delegated financial powers that no single department could access on its own.

**What it looked like.** Prior tribal-area postings had shown the collector that "when I speak in their language ... suddenly you see a different [dynamic] — people are listening in their own language." She personally chaired the stakeholder meeting that decided which language to prioritise, personally addressed a 150-person community meeting to answer "why are we doing this," and stayed hands-on enough to describe the district's biggest continuing frustration in the present tense.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The deployment needs to converge multiple government departments and community trust simultaneously, and no single technical team can unlock either alone.
- **Fails when:** The effort sits entirely within one department's mandate and doesn't require cross-department data or community mobilisation.

## Before → after

- **Before:** Not documented in the source
- **After:** The collector personally chaired the stakeholder meeting that decided which language to prioritise and personally addressed a 150-person community meeting to answer "why are we doing this."

## Why it is reusable

It tells the next adopter what to secure before any recording or model work: a champion with the authority to converge departments and release funds, not only a technical owner. The pathway's own gaps note that Scale-stage funding beyond the champion is not documented. It is a suggestion from another adoption's lived experience, to be checked against the condition tag above.
