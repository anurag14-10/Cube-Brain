## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Use AI because a single farmer question routinely requires weather, soil, crop, and scheme data assembled and answered in seconds.

**Why:** No human extension system can perform that synthesis at the moment of query; this is the specific failure mode AI addresses, not a general efficiency argument.
