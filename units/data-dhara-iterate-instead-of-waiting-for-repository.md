## Unit

According to the Data DHARA pathway's account of Nivesh Suvidha (Invest UP), this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Build iteratively as deployment revealed gaps, rather than waiting for a complete centralised policy repository; drive the work through in-person engagement with leadership (the CEO as central decision-maker), building confidence in the broader vision rather than positioning it as a standalone technical deliverable.

**Alternative considered.** Waiting for a complete centralised policy repository; positioning the work as a standalone technical deliverable.

**Why.** The document attributes the project's velocity to these two choices.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** No complete, centralised policy repository exists — the document names poor access to appropriate data, with no central repositories or ownership structures, as a drawback of AI adoption at state scale.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Before → after

- **Before:** Not documented in the source
- **After:** MoU (November 2025) → SOW (January 2026) → live pilot with 20 Udyami Mitras (~March 2026): roughly 3–4 months.
