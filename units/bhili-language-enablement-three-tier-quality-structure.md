## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation's Bhili Language Enablement pathway, the asset is a standing three-layer quality structure: field contributors and participants at the base, checkers/reviewers in the middle, and subject-matter validators and linguistic experts at the top — with all three layers working simultaneously rather than validation happening only after collection finishes.

**Purpose.** Catches quality problems — mixed-dialect speech, contributors who don't actually know the language well, inconsistent translations — while collection is still happening, instead of discovering them in a finished dataset.

**Reuse condition.** Applies where local government departments already have credible, trusted speakers of the target language on staff. The tier structure is reusable as-is, together with the recruitment principle behind it: contributors were sourced through local government departments (agriculture, education, health, revenue), not an external vendor's hiring pipeline, because the administration already knew who was fluent, literate and trusted locally.

**What it looked like.** The taluka's own agriculture assistant officer became the single named validator for the entire district's data, personally building a glossary of roughly 3,000 words and catching cases where a recorded "Bhili" speaker was mixing in Marathi or a neighbouring dialect (Mavchi) closely enough to need exclusion from the dataset. The field contributor team of 22–25 was selected from a roughly 70-person taluka agriculture staff pool. The pathway's coverage note folds the Pilot-stage validator and dialect friction into this unit.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The language has meaningful dialect variation, and credible, trusted contributors are more easily found through existing local institutions than through an open call.
- **Fails when:** No local administrative network already touches speakers of the target language, or the language has no meaningful internal dialect variation to validate against.

## Why it is reusable

The structure and the recruitment channel can be lifted directly by another district or language effort. The pathway's own example also shows its limit: one named validator carried the whole district's data. It is a suggestion from another adoption's lived experience, to be checked against the condition tag above.
