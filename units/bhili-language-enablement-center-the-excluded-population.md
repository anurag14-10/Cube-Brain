## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation's Bhili Language Enablement pathway (Project Astitva, Nandurbar), this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Frame the effort around a named excluded person and the service they can't access, not around the language as a technical artifact.

**Alternative considered.** Approach the project as "build Bhili AI resources" for the language generally.

**Why.** The district collector had earlier served in a different tribal posting and had already seen that speaking to people in their own language changes the dynamic of a service interaction. Marathi-only advisory left tribal women — who travel less and had the least Marathi exposure of anyone in the community — most cut off from agriculture, health and education schemes.

**What it looked like.** The collector's framing, as the pathway quotes it: "Let's be careful when we say low-resource languages. They are not low-resource cultures. They are not low-resource communities. They are low-resource in our digital imagination."

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The population most excluded by a digital service is identifiable and named, not a diffuse "everyone who speaks language X."
- **Fails when:** The language effort has no specific service or excluded user in mind — the framework's related caution applies, "don't begin with 'let's build a model for this language.'"

## Before → after

- **Before:** Marathi-only advisory left tribal women — who travel less and had the least Marathi exposure in the community — most cut off from agriculture, health and education schemes.
- **After:** The project's stated aim became reaching "the last mile of society", with women in tribal communities named as the specific group at greatest risk of exclusion.

## Why it is reusable

It changes where a language effort starts: with the person who is excluded and the service they are entitled to, rather than with a dataset or model for the language. It is a suggestion from another adoption's lived experience, to be checked against the condition tag above.
