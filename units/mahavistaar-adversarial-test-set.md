## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the asset is: a safety-testing bank of 500 attack patterns covering in-scope, out-of-scope, sensitive/distress, abuse, complaint, and jailbreak scenarios, maintained as a living document.

**Purpose:** Makes the system refuse well, not just answer well, in a domain where wrong answers carry institutional risk.

**Reuse condition:** Via the OpenAgriNet ecosystem (contact EkStep Foundation), for agricultural advisory contexts specifically.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The goal is refusing well, not just answering well, in a domain where wrong answers carry institutional risk.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

A ready test bank shortens pre-launch safety testing for agricultural advisory adopters and models the categories others should cover.
