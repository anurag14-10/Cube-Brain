## Unit

According to the Bharat Vistaar pathway contributed by COSS (Centre for Open Societal Systems), this is a toolkit asset from the deployment.

**What it is for.** The governance template — institutional ownership structure, data accountability model, content authority assignment — that travels with the open-source OAN code. The architecture and template transfer; localised agricultural knowledge bases, language models and integration with country-specific agronomy and extension systems are rebuilt at each deployment.

**Reuse condition.** Use when replicating the deployment across states or countries; the template must travel with the code, not be redesigned locally.

No file or link is attached — the document names the asset but does not publish it.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Federating across geographically or institutionally distinct deployments.
- **Fails when:** A single institution controls the full deployment (the template overhead is then unnecessary); or no research institution equivalent to ICAR exists to ground advisory in verified agronomic knowledge, or no national farmer identity registry equivalent to AgriStack is available to give AI agents farmer context.
