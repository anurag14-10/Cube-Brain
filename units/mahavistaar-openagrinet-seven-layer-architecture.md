## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the asset is: the foundational open-source architecture — Knowledge Engine, Memory Layer, Trust Layer, Agent Core, Action Gateway, Reach Layer, Learning Layer.

**Purpose:** Gives a new deployment a DPG-licensed architectural starting point instead of designing the full layer stack from scratch.

**Reuse condition:** Freely available under DPG licence at openagri.net.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Any agricultural AI deployment federating multiple institutional data sources (per the pathway's toolkit table).
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

An adopter can start from a published layer stack rather than designing one, which is a large part of why later builds compressed from nine months to weeks.
