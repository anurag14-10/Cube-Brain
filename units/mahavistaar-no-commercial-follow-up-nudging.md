## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Replace 'Would you like to know more?' with a lighter close, or nothing at all.

**Why:** A commercial engagement pattern reads oddly on a public service line — answering the question and stopping is often better.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The service is a public institutional advisory line, not a commercial engagement product.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Before → after

- **Before:** Not documented in the source
- **After:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

It flags that conversation patterns copied from commercial bots can undermine tone on an institutional line.
