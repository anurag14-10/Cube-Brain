## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: The system must speak as the institution itself, not as an AI in its own right.

**Why:** Farmer trust and accountability need to sit with a recognisable public institution, not a technology brand — a farmer who gets bad advice experiences it as the institution failing her, not the model.

**In this deployment:** The bot was named Vasudha and speaks explicitly as the Department of Agriculture, Maharashtra.

## Why it is reusable

Choosing who the system speaks as decides who must own its answers; adopters can make this choice explicitly instead of defaulting to a tech brand.
