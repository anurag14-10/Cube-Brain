## Unit

According to CEEW's Climate Intelligence pathway, this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Treat AI deployment as an ongoing partnership with implementation partners rather than a procurement exercise.

**Alternative considered.** Procuring the system as conventional software.

**Why.** Both systems continued to evolve after deployment as models improved and institutional needs changed; long-term ownership requires data stewardship, governance, monitoring, technical expertise and ongoing collaboration with implementation partners.

**Context.** For CRAVIS, the partnership meant continuously expanding datasets and refining user interactions; for the early-warning system, validating forecasts against real-world outcomes, incorporating additional datasets and strengthening institutional confidence over successive cycles.
