## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation / People+AI's Voice AI Adoption Barriers pathway, this is a strategic decision drawn from the organisations it synthesises.

**Decision.** Write down the specific problem, the specific user, and what success looks like in measurable terms — before evaluating any vendor, and independently of what any vendor's demo can do.

**Alternative considered.** Joining a subsidised programme or piloting a vendor first, on the assumption the right use case will become clear along the way.

**Why.** Sesame Workshop India joined a subsidised programme without a defined problem to solve — their actual bottleneck (reminding field workers to upload data) turned out not to need voice AI at all; an SMS reminder would have worked. The inverse held for Pradical Works, who defined connect rate, talk-time distribution, and lead conversion as success metrics before a single call was made.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** An organisation has vendor access or funding available and is tempted to start there.
- **Fails when:** The use case is already sharply defined and this step would just be paperwork repeating known facts.

## Before → after

- **Before:** Sesame Workshop India, starting from a subsidised programme without a defined problem, found its actual bottleneck did not need voice AI — an SMS reminder would have worked.
- **After:** Pradical Works, with connect rate, talk-time distribution and lead conversion defined before the first call, produced pilot data measurably cleaner than every other pilot in the dataset.

## Why it is reusable

The condition tag says when this choice held across the organisations synthesised; the before→after shows what the other choice cost. It is a suggestion from other adoptions' lived experience, not a recommendation.
