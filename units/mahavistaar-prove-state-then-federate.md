## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Sequence national federation (Bharat-VISTAAR) only after the state-level architecture is proven at scale, rather than launching both together.

**Why:** An unproven architecture federated too early risks propagating failure modes nationally before they're understood locally.

**In this deployment:** Bharat-VISTAAR was announced in the Union Budget 2026–27, after MahaVISTAAR's state-level system was already proven.
