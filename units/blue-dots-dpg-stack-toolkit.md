## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation's Blue Dots pathway, this is a toolkit asset listed in the pathway's toolkit table: the seven-block DPG architecture (Knowledge/Memory/Trust/Agent/Action/Reach/Learning layers) plus the Signal, Aggregator and Facilitator DPGs.

**Purpose:** Ready building blocks for local supply-demand discovery: seven layers (Knowledge, Memory, Trust, Agent, Action, Reach, Learning) plus the Signal DPG (turns each voice interaction into a location-anchored Blue Dot and manages matching), the Aggregator DPG (institutions onboard participants at scale) and the Facilitator DPG (district administrations see the supply-demand gap).

**Reuse condition:** Open-source, model- and speech-provider-agnostic; applies to any district or sector attempting local supply-demand discovery, not only jobs.
