## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Size the gap between the proposed AI use case and the current state — including doing nothing — before committing. That gap is the value being created.

**Alternative considered:** Committing to AI on the basis of what the technology can do, without a sized comparison against the current state.

**Why:** A human-only advisory model scales with staff; an AI model scales with usage. Where the staffing constraint is fixed, that difference is the case for AI.

**In this deployment:** Maharashtra's ~1 field officer per 2,000 farmers was the concrete constraint that made the case.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** A genuine current-state baseline exists and a limiting constraint can be named concretely.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source
