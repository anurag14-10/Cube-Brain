## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Treat architecture, governance frameworks, language-pipeline methodology, and the failure-mode library as the transferable unit — not the trust relationship with farmers.

**In this deployment:** Ethiopia's ATI and Amul/Sarlaben both reused the architecture; Ethiopia's team needed to locally adapt farmer-trust mechanics rather than import them directly.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The receiving context can adapt an existing architecture rather than construct one from scratch.
- **Fails when:** Farmer-trust mechanics are assumed to transfer with the architecture.

## Before → after

- **Before:** Maharashtra's build took nine months, from scratch.
- **After:** Ethiopia's, drawing on that architecture, took three months; Amul's took three weeks.

## Why it is reusable

It tells a next adopter what they can import and what they must budget to rebuild locally.
