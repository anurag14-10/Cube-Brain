## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the decision was: Position extension officers as owning the relationship, with the system handling the off-hours/on-demand question.

**Why:** Workforce trust and adoption depend on staff not perceiving the system as a threat to their role.
