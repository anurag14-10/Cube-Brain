## Unit

According to EkStep Foundation's Bhili Language Enablement pathway, this is the district's own published replication handbook — written explicitly for another district or language to pick up, not as an internal report.

**Steps (in order):**

- 1. Assess Needs — a local assessment of linguistic and agricultural priorities.
- 2. Build Partnerships — engage local NGOs, cooperatives and the agriculture department.
- 3. Develop Content & Tools — adapt advisory content into the local language using voice-based tools.
- 4. Deploy & Train — launch the platform and train field staff and local resource persons.
- Gate: the handbook puts quality over speed, and validation and use-case testing before wider expansion; gate criteria between individual steps are not documented in the source.

**Common failure mode.** Treating the four steps as a race rather than a gate at each stage.

**Where it is being used.** The same playbook is already committed to three further languages (Mathwadi Bhili, Mavchi, Pawari), with Bodo and Garo named as later candidates. No other adopter's live deployment is yet documented.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** The next adopter has, or can build, the same kind of convening authority (a district-level or equivalent administrative anchor) this playbook assumes at step 2.
- **Fails when:** No equivalent local administrative convening authority exists to execute steps 2 and 4 — the playbook's steps assume that authority is already in place, not that it needs to be created from nothing.

## Why it is reusable

The order of the steps and the instruction not to rush past validation are the reusable part; the condition tag says the sequence presumes a convening authority already exists. It is a suggestion from another adoption's lived experience, to be checked against the condition tag above.
