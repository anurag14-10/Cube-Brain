## Unit

According to the Bharat Vistaar pathway contributed by COSS (Centre for Open Societal Systems), this is a strategic decision from the deployment.

**Decision.** Map all required ecosystem roles — technology, domain, data, language, delivery, financing, research — and assign a named organisation to each before architecture work begins.

**Alternative considered.** Begin building with core partners named, and resolve remaining roles as needed during development.

**Why.** A language-model gap discovered mid-build forces a delay or a compromise; a data partnership not formalised before deployment becomes an informal agreement that breaks under operational pressure. A single unnamed role in a chain of 30 is enough to stall a launch.

**Context.** Named partners span the solution side (COSS, EkStep, AI4Bharat, Bhashini, Vassar Labs, IISc, IIT Bombay, Samagra, VoiceEra, India AI Mission, Karya, Artha Global, telephony providers), the provider side (ICAR, agricultural universities, market data institutions, Maharashtra livestock and fisheries departments) and the community/ecosystem side (Gates Foundation, World Bank, UNDP).

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Any deployment requiring more than 5 distinct external partners or data sources.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Before → after

- **Before:** Not documented in the source
- **After:** 30+ organisations across solution and shared capabilities, provider institutions, community and ecosystem, and sponsor/owner roles were all resolved before the national instance launched.

## Why it is reusable

The condition tag says when this choice held in the source deployment; the alternative and the before→after show what a different choice would trade away. It is a suggestion from another adoption's lived experience, not a recommendation.
