## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the asset is: data-sharing MOU templates, consent architecture, and an institutional ownership model, validated under India's DPDP Act.

**Purpose:** Gives a new deployment a starting legal/governance template for negotiating external data-sharing agreements, instead of drafting from zero.

**Reuse condition:** Via EkStep Foundation; explicitly requires customisation for specific departmental structures.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Reused via EkStep Foundation and customised to the specific departmental structures — the pathway says that customisation is explicitly required.
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

Starting templates shorten the legal side of federated data-sharing for Indian adopters.
