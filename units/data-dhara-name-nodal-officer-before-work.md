## Unit

According to the Data DHARA pathway's account of Nivesh Suvidha (Invest UP), this is a failure and fix from the deployment.

**Failure.** No single nodal officer was appointed for data sourcing at the outset, and there was no internal digital-transformation leadership to own the AI agenda — decisions and momentum depended entirely on the CEO. Sourcing required chasing multiple stakeholders, and coordination between the institutional team and the vendor needed more deliberate structure than was in place, demanding significant orchestration from a neutral organisation (EkStep).

**Fix.** For the next use case (stated as what to do differently, not yet recorded as applied): request a designated nodal officer (SME) upfront; identify all data and access requirements and get owner timeline commitments before work begins; invest in in-person engagement with both the working team and the CEO early, not only at signing and final demo.

**Insight.** A project that depends entirely on one senior sponsor has a fragile foundation. The 'data problem' is an institutional-alignment and process-redesign problem — data pipelines and APIs are necessary but not sufficient.
