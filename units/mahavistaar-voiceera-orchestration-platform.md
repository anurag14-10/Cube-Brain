## Unit

According to the MahaVISTAAR pathway contributed by the Department of Agriculture (Government of Maharashtra), EkStep Foundation and OpenAgriNet, the asset is: a model-agnostic, language-agnostic, telephony-provider-agnostic orchestration platform.

**Purpose:** Connects models into a deployable, maintainable system — the orchestration layer, not another model, is what most deployments actually lack.

**Reuse condition:** Via the OpenAgriNet ecosystem; applies specifically to Indian telephony infrastructure.

## Condition

- **Applies when:** Indian telephony infrastructure (the pathway states the platform applies specifically to it).
- **Fails when:** Not documented in the source

## Why it is reusable

It is a liftable orchestration layer for adopters who already have usable models but no deployable system.
