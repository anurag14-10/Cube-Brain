## Responsibilities

From the Product Charter: the Program Team ("David, Anupama + Team") owns stakeholder communication and the pathway content lifecycle (reviewing and publishing contributor submissions). It onboards new adopters and relays user feedback to the [Solution Team](solution-team.md).

In the app, that review-and-publish job is the admin half of [two-step publish](../features/two-step-publish.md) and the [contributor registration approval](../workflows/contributor-registration-approval.md), done on `/admin` under the [admin](../roles/admin.md) role. The toolkit-asset requirement names "Admins / Program Team" as the people who review assets together with their pathway. The charter does not name an organisation for this team.

## Cadence

A weekly cross-team review with the Solution Team, and a formal milestone review with senior management at each milestone boundary.
