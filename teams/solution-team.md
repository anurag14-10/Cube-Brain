## Responsibilities

From the Product Charter's execution model: the Solution Team ("Kamesh + Tekdi Team") owns solution functionality, UI/UX and the data and technical architecture. It builds and maintains the web app and the planned Claude Skill integration, and handles product and technical user issues. It owns the app's features (see `features/`).

Members are recorded only where the charter or the registry states them: [Kamesh](../people/kamesh.md) and the Tekdi developer [Anurag Goutam](../people/anurag-goutam.md). Other code authors in the history, [Abhiram Gooty](../people/abhiram-gooty.md) and [Swanand Kulkarni](../people/swanand-kulkarni.md), have no documented team, so they are not listed as members.

## Cadence

A weekly cross-team review with the [Program Team](program-team.md) against the current milestone, and a formal milestone review with senior management at each milestone boundary. Requirements and feedback are tracked in tabs inside the charter document itself, not in an external ticket tracker.
