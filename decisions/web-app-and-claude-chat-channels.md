## Context

The Product Charter's scope covers exploring pathways, analysing one's own adoption, and contributing and curating pathways.

## Decision

- **Web app**: the primary interface for Explore, Analyse and Contribute.
- **Claude Chat**: supports Explore, Analyse and Contribute without visiting the web app.
- **Other channels**: not yet explored or defined.

## Alternatives considered

None are recorded in the charter.

## Consequences

- The Claude channel is judged at [EkStep Contribution via Claude](../milestones/ekstep-contribution-via-claude.md) (2026-12-15).
- Further channels wait for [Channel Expansion](../milestones/channel-expansion.md) (2027-03-15).
- The charter gives no decision date or decider, so `decided_on` and `decided_by` are blank.
